> **Example research entry:** This text is a template prompt, not original research findings.

For industrial environments, useful visibility balances safety, operational constraints, and system ownership. A real study might state its environment, authorization, data handling, and limits before drawing conclusions.

### Questions worth documenting

- Which observations are passive and which change system behavior?
- What context is required to interpret an anomaly responsibly?
- How are findings communicated to operators and engineers?
