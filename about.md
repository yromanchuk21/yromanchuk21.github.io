I’m Yakov Vasilenko-Romanchuk, a mechanical engineer (BS, CSU Sacramento) with a Certificate in Scientific Computing from the Physics Department. As a field service engineer for industrial sorting machines, I maintain, calibrate, and align laser and hyperspectral camera systems, write sorting recipes and program hardware when a job needs it, and deploy new machines on new production lines. I also have two years of IT network and server administration experience across Active Directory, Linux servers, and Ubiquiti enterprise networks. Outside work, I built and threat-modeled an [environmental monitoring system](/projects/environmental-monitoring-system/) using ESP32-C3 sensor nodes and a Raspberry Pi 4 server communicating over MQTT on an isolated VLAN. I found that the shared node credentials allowed a compromised device to impersonate any other device, so I replaced them with per-device certificates and implemented ACLs to restrict each node to its own topic. Hardware hacking drew me into cybersecurity, so I'm working through the Hack The Box Junior Cybersecurity Analyst pathway. My goal is to combine my knowledge of physical machines, networking and embedded systems in an operational technology (OT) and industrial control systems (ICS) security role.
