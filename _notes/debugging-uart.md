> **Example note:** Adapt this to your own authorized lab practice.

Before changing firmware or wiring, document voltage levels, shared ground, pin mapping, baud rate, capture settings, and the known-good state. The fastest debugging sessions are usually the ones with the clearest baseline.

```text
[ ] authorized target and documented scope
[ ] common ground confirmed
[ ] level compatibility confirmed
[ ] capture settings recorded
[ ] original state preserved
```
